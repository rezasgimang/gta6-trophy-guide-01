Road to the Platinum

Roughly 200 hours, difficulty about 4/10. Play in this order and nothing gets left behind.

  1. Phase 1 — Chapter 1, do it now. Meditate on Lucia’s balcony before Chapter 1 ends. It is the one spot that may not come back. Keep a separate Chapter 1 save as insurance.
  2. Phase 2 — Play the story, Chapters 1–4. All 12 story trophies pop as you go. Take the time-sensitive stranger race “Race Against Yago” in Chapter 3 when it appears.
  3. Phase 3 — Collect as you travel. Grab collectible cards, WAiNK It at new places, register new vehicles to Scooter Bros, and drive everything you have not driven yet.
  4. Phase 4 — Strangers and activities. Finish 8 stranger strands (including the six character finales), then the races, shooting range, fishing, bowling, arcade and drone races.
  5. Phase 5 — Cleanup, then 100%. Robs, laundering, spending and challenges after the finale. 100% completion is the last trophy — and the platinum pops with it.
⚠ One potentially missable trophy: Positive Mental Attitude — meditate at every spot in Leonida. Lucia’s Chapter 1 balcony meditation may disappear after Chapter 1, and there is no mission replay. Do it in Chapter 1 and keep a Chapter 1 save. Whether that one spot is strictly required is not yet proven — the safe play is to do it anyway.

There are 0 online trophies — everything in GTA VI is single-player. No online play is needed for the platinum.
